<div class="playmat-process">
<h2>How a commission works</h2>
<p class="playmat-process-lead">We'll work with you to design your playmat and settle on a design you like before anything goes off to print.</p>
<div class="playmat-phases">
<div class="playmat-phase">
<span class="playmat-phase-label">Step 1</span>
<h3>Brief</h3>
<p>Send your ideas, references, and any text or motifs you want on the mat. We will confirm details and timeline by email and send a payment link for 50% of the cost.</p>
</div>
<div class="playmat-phase">
<span class="playmat-phase-label">Step 2</span>
<h3>First look</h3>
<p>We'll send you an initial design for you to review. Let us know what you think so we can keep what works, make changes, or go in a different direction.</p>
</div>
<div class="playmat-phase">
<span class="playmat-phase-label">Step 3</span>
<h3>Revisions</h3>
<p>We'll update the design based on your feedback. You get a second round of feedback to lock in the final version. If more changes are required after that we'll give you a quote to finish it off.</p>
</div>
<div class="playmat-phase">
<span class="playmat-phase-label">Step 4</span>
<h3>Print</h3>
<p>Once you sign off, the mat goes to print! Collect from the Gibraltar Warhammer Club or arrange for local delivery.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
