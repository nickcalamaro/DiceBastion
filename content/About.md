**Dice Bastion is a part of the Gibraltar Warhammer Club.** The Gibraltar Warhammer Club was founded in 2016 as Gibraltar's primary club for all things Warhammer; it has since evolved into Gibraltar's home for all tabletop games.

Our venue offers a board game library, comfortable seating, and gaming tables, providing a space for players to meet, compete, and enjoy a wide variety of games.

We run regular events, shared through WhatsApp and our social media, and welcome the public every week.

Members receive exclusive benefits, including local discounts and free access to the venue. Your membership helps keep the club running by covering bills, improving the space with new furniture and games, and supporting the events we host.

## Who we are

<div class="donate-about-grid">

<div class="donate-about-item">

<h3>Gibraltar Warhammer Club</h3>

<p>Founded in 2016, the Gibraltar Warhammer Club is the organisation who run the club we all know and love at 23 Casemates Vaults.</p>

<p>We are a Ministry of Culture registered organisation who run on behalf of our paying members. The club elects a committee each year who manage the administration of the club, organise events, and ensure we have a safe, inclusive space for everyone to play their favourite games.</p>

</div>

<div class="donate-about-item">

<img src="/img/sn-battle-reports-logo.png" alt="SN Battle Reports" class="donate-about-logo" width="80" height="80">

<h3><a href="https://snbattlereports.com/">SN Battle Reports</a></h3>

<p>SN Battle Reports are a YouTube channel founded by Steven Pardo in 2014. In addition to their channel, SN organise massive events that attract players from all around the world to play Warhammer on the Rock.</p>

<p>SN were crucial to getting the club set up and are still the Club's main sponsors, funding many of the improvements and covering rent when our membership hasn't been able to cover it.</p>

<p>SN has their own SN+ Membership and Patreon available.</p>

</div>

<div class="donate-about-item">

<img src="/img/childline/Club%20logo.png" alt="Dice Bastion" class="donate-about-logo" width="80" height="80">

<h3>Dice Bastion</h3>

<p>Founded in 2025 by Jen Luttig and Nicky Calamaro, Dice Bastion is a tabletop gaming brand that supports and grows the hobby on the Rock through regular TCG tournaments, Board Game Nights, RPG events, and charity drives. They run a tabletop gaming blog and partner with other hobby groups in Gibraltar and Spain to keep growing the hobby.</p>

<p>Dice Bastion are currently sponsoring and hosting the bookings website for events, tables, and memberships for the GWC.</p>

</div>

Any Gibraltar Warhammer Club or Dice Bastion membership are interchangeable and provide the same benefits.
