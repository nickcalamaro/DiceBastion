We've been running **Magic the Gathering** at the club for years, and it's still one of the busiest things we do. If you're looking for MTG in Gibraltar (drafts, pre-releases, or just a table and some packs), this is the page for you.

We play at the Gibraltar Warhammer Club **Unit 23a Casemates Vaults**, Casemates Square. We're open for games every **Friday from 6pm**. If you'd like to support us you can also check out our [membership options](/memberships/) to get discounts on our events and book tables every day of the week.

## Events

Beyond Friday open nights, we host drafts, pre-releases and tournaments which you can find on our [events calendar](/events/).

If you're new to the club, Friday open night is the easiest way in. Bring a deck if you've got one, or ask when you arrive. All skill levels are welcome, our group covers all bases from people who've been playing Magic since the 90s, to new players just building their first decks.
