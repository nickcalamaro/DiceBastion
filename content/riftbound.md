**Riftbound** has a proper home at Dice Bastion. If you want to play in Gibraltar (Nexus Nights, casual games, or finding other local players), you're in the right place.

We run sessions at **Unit 23a Casemates Vaults**, Casemates Square. On **Thursdays from 6pm** we're open to everyone. Members can book tables on other days too.

## Events

We currently run Riftbound every Thursday at the [Gibraltar Warhammer Club](/about/). We also have regular tournaments and pre-releases so make sure to check out our [events calendar](/events/) to see what else is coming up.

Whether you're a new player or already have a tuned deck, we'd love for you to come join us and help grow the local scene for this awesome TCG!
