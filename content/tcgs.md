Dice Bastion is Gibraltar’s hub for **trading card games**. We run regular events for all kinds of TCGs. We also stock booster boxes, packs and accessories via our online [Dice Bastion shop](https://shop.dicebastion.com).

## Games

- **[Magic the Gathering](/magic-the-gathering/)** - Come join us for Friday open nights for some casual play, or check out our [events page](/events/) for upcoming drafts, pre-releases and tournaments.
- **[Riftbound](/riftbound/)** - Come join us each Thursday for our local Nexus Nights.
- **One Piece TCG**, **Gundam TCG**, **Pokemon TCG** - We've got players for a wide range of games! Come on down to the club on our Friday open nights to see what's being played or get in contact to join our local TCG WhatsApp group and find some players for your favourite game.

Whatever your choice of game, you'll find a welcoming community here. You can also [book out a table](/bookings/) via the Gibraltar Warhammer Club to play at our unique venue!

## Upcoming events

We host TCG events pretty much every week of the year! Check our [events page](/events/) to see what's on the calendar and save your spot!

We run events regularly at the Gibraltar Warhammer Club. Riftbound Nexus Nights are on **Thursdays from 6pm**, and we're open every **Friday from 6pm** for Magic and other games.
